Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how VoxMemo ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our voice recording and transcription application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and authentication credentials (via Supabase Auth)

Audio and transcription data

  • Voice recordings you create in the app are sent to Deepgram for transcription
  • Audio is processed in real time and not permanently stored on our servers
  • Transcribed text and generated documents are stored locally on your device (Hive database)

Usage data

  • Minutes recorded per day (for plan limit enforcement)
  • Number of sessions (for trial limit tracking)
  • Monthly aggregate API usage (for cost monitoring)

Device fingerprint

To prevent trial abuse, we collect a non-personally-identifiable device fingerprint (combination of hardware identifiers on Android; vendor identifier on iOS). This is used solely to enforce trial limits across reinstalls. It does not identify you personally and cannot be used to track you.

Subscription data

  • Purchase and subscription status (managed by RevenueCat and the platform app store)
  • We do not store payment card or financial details

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide transcription and AI documentation services
  • To enforce plan limits (trial, student, pro)
  • To manage your subscription and account
  • To monitor service availability and prevent abuse

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

VoxMemo uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — authentication and database (EU/US infrastructure). Privacy Policy
  • Deepgram — speech-to-text transcription. Audio is sent to Deepgram's API for processing. Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini — AI text generation from transcripts. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management. Privacy Policy

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing their data handling.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Transcriptions and documents are stored locally on your device
  • Usage counters and account data are stored in Supabase with row-level security
  • Audio files are not retained on our servers after transcription
  • All API communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS

5. Data Retention

  • Local session data: retained until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Usage logs: retained for billing and abuse prevention purposes
  • Device fingerprint: retained as long as your account exists, or until subscription is active
  • Account data: retained until you request deletion

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your locally stored transcriptions at any time (PDF export)

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

VoxMemo is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the app or by updating the date at the top of this page.
